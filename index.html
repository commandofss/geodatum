GeoDatum

A small model that calls a verified converter for Nigerian survey coordinates, and an honest look at where it still fails to ask. Tool calls went from 6 of 56 to 56 of 56 correct; asking when the datum or grid is missing did not improve (3 of 44 before, 0 of 44 after).

Main negative result

After training the model starts every reply with a tool call. It guesses UTM 31N when the grid is missing, fills in Minna when the datum is missing, and copies an unsupported UTM 33N straight from the user. Two causes are untested: the loss covered every token (assistant_only_loss=False), and 75 steps on 600 rows may be too little.

What didn’t work

  • A first 200-row adapter looped: it ended with <|END_RESPONSE|> and began a new turn, and the scorer didn’t stop on that token.
  • The first prompt said “Nigeria Mid Belt” while the tool expects “Mid Belt”, so the model copied the wrong name.
  • A first AuditKIT run reused a cached result because the two runs had identical fingerprints; we caught it by cross-checking against our own scorer.

Limits and next steps

Limits

  • Synthetic, templated, in-distribution data
  • Minna datum only
  • “Inside Nigeria” is a bounding box
  • 600 of 2,000 rows, 100 test rows, one run
  • No real users yet

To test next

  • Loss on the reply only
  • Train longer on all 2,000 rows
  • Contrast pairs: same coordinates with and without the grid
  • Reward-based training with the converter as the reward
  • A check outside the model for missing inputs
